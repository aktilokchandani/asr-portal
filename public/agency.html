Overview

ASR's business at a glance.

Two divisions, one pipeline

Staffing: a client company posts a job request → ASR matches it against the private candidate pool → ASR assigns a candidate to the client as a placement, setting the agency markup → ASR invoices the client per period worked.

Outsourced Bookkeeping & Advisory: a client signs up for ongoing service instead of a hire → ASR sets up an engagement (optionally staffed by someone from the candidate pool) → ASR bills a flat monthly fee. Candidates and clients never interact directly or see each other's contact details — every match runs through the agency.
